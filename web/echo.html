Dana, on her own Sudor

Dana is a crew member. Her medicines and conditions are in Sudor, a separate add-on on a separate server, and never leave it.

  1. Open Sudor on a screen in another tab.
  2. Say who she is there, then "I just started a roofing job, seven to five."
  3. Say "Share my working day with my crew." It reads out eight digits.
  4. Come back here, type the digits under The people on the crew, and ask about the job again.

Orbita then asks Sudor, server to server, how her day looks. What comes back is how hot her shift gets, when it turns risky for her, and how many hours of heat she should do. Never why.
